Persian with Simorghak is a cartoon series for children of Persian-speaking families living abroad: kids who understand Persian but answer in English.
In Episode 1, meet Tara, her little brother Arash, their grandmother Maman-joon in Isfahan, and Simorghak, the magical little bird from Maman-joon's Shahnameh book.
New episodes every Saturday on Settle in UK.
